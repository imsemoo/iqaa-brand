Arabic

Reserved, with its rules

Arabic is not set in this round. When it is, the partner is chosen the way Atkinson was: on real product text, side by side, measured.

  • Shortlist: Alexandria, Readex Pro, Vazirmatn, Rubik, Noto Sans Arabic.
  • Excluded: IBM Plex Sans Arabic, Cairo, Noto Kufi Arabic (used by a competitor), Tajawal.
  • It must match Atkinson’s weight at 16 px and hold tabular figures for times and codes.
  • Display line-height at least 1.4; no tashkeel in interface text; letter-spacing never applied to Arabic.
