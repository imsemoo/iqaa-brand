Proportion

How much of each

Ground ~80%Lavender ~6%Ink ~11%Violet ≤ 3%

Not a quota, a consequence of the rules: violet marks only what people did or must do, and one primary action. If violet passes a few percent of a surface, something that is not a person has been coloured.
