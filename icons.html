Icons

Steps, stations and actions

One stroke family on a 24 px grid, as heavy as the text it sits beside. The step icons draw the line’s own strokes: a loop is a task for a person, an arch waits, a lifted pen is a pause.

Construction

Grid and stroke

Grid
24 px, 2 px margin: the live area is 20 px.
Stroke
1.5 px: the stem of Atkinson Regular at 16 px (1.50 px), rounded to a quarter pixel. An icon is as heavy as the label beside it.
Ends and joins
Round. Corners 2–3 px; curves as round as the pen’s.
Sizes
24 in navigation, 20 beside 14–16 px text. Never filled; the one dot is the pause, as in a line.
Never
Gears, plugs, lightning, nodes and wires, sparkles. Settings is sliders; Watar is its waiting arch; a station is a ring only on the map, as in the Line screen.
