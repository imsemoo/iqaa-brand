Mark

A line that signs its own name

Iqaa’s mark is three steps of its own line, drawn with one pen: it arrives straight, a hand loops it once, and it leaves rising. Every organization’s line is written with the same pen.

Construction

Everything is a measure of one step

One step
24 units. The mark is three steps of Iqaa’s own line: a run, a loop, a rising finish.
The pen
4.6 units in the mark, 3.2 in a line. The mark’s pen is heavier so it holds at 16 px.
The loop
Rises 30 units, a step and a quarter. In the lockup it stands exactly as tall as the capitals.
The finish
Leaves the loop and rises 16 units over 18: the line goes on, in time.
Colour
Ink for the strokes that run by themselves; violet for the loop, the person.
Clear space
One step all round. Nothing enters it.

Wordmark

Iqaa, written with the mark’s pen

Atkinson Hyperlegible Next at weight 440, outlined so it never depends on an installed font. In the lockup the mark sits on the word’s baseline and its loop rises exactly to the cap height; at that size the letters’ stem equals the mark’s pen, so the name and the stroke are written with one hand. The serifed I keeps the name from reading lqaa.

Clear space

One step of room all round

The dashed field is one step on every side, measured from the mark at the size it is used. Nothing enters it: not a headline, not an edge, not another logo.
