Guidelines

Ten rules keep it whole

The rules that, kept, make every surface recognisably Iqaa — and the files, tokens and checks that go with them.

Rules

Ten rules

  1. Violet is a person.Work done by hand, a step waiting for someone, the button someone presses. Nothing automatic is ever violet.
  2. The stroke carries the state.A straight runs by itself, an arch waits, a loop is done by hand, a dot is a pause. Colour is never the only signal.
  3. The mark is Iqaa’s own line.Three steps of its line, drawn with the same pen as every signature. Never redraw, reverse or decorate it.
  4. One beat is the unit.A step is one beat of the line; 16 px of space and 600 ms of time. Every size and duration divides it.
  5. Signatures are true.They come from a real line or a labelled demo, and they appear only where that line is the subject.
  6. The organization leads.On its subdomain, links and messages, its name comes first; Iqaa signs off with Runs on Iqaa.
  7. Ink and white carry the page.Cool lavender greys for quiet grounds. No cream, no gradient, no glass, no glow.
  8. Watar drafts; people decide.Its work always waits under an arch for a person, with what it did and what it will not do.
  9. Motion has a cause.Step, hold, release, on the tempo. Nothing loops or floats on its own.
  10. No fact is invented.No customer, number, price or integration until it exists. Brackets until then.

Quality

Checked before handing over

✓Every text pair passes WCAG 2.2 AAComputed for every pair from the tokens; the matrix is on the colour page.
✓Every state reads without colourStraight, arch, loop, dot and snap, rendered in greyscale and through a deuteranopia filter on the signature page.
✓The mark holds at 16 pxThe pen is drawn heavier below 48 px so it stays 1.6 px wide; seen on light and dark tabs.
✓One pen for the mark and the lettersWordmark weight 440 solved so the stem equals the mark’s pen at cap height.
✓Fonts are measured, not pickedMeasured on the font files: tabular figures, Turkish, I and l, x-height, width.
✓No invented factsNames, addresses, prices and roles are in brackets; template steps are labelled illustrative.
✓No banned codesNo gradient, glass, glow, sparkle, wire, gear or music sign anywhere in the files.
✓Pages work at 375 pxChecked in the browser at 375, 768 and 1280 px; no sideways scroll.
