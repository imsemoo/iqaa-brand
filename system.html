Watar and co-branding

The assistant, the packs, and whose name leads

Iqaa is infrastructure for other organizations’ work. Its assistant drafts and waits; its packs are descriptors; and on everything an organization’s clients see, the organization comes first.

Watar

The waiting arch

Watar

Watar (وتر) is the string of an oud: a line that sounds only when a hand strikes it. Its mark is the waiting arch — a string drawn up and waiting — because everything Watar makes, a drafted line, a job filled from a WhatsApp message, a suggested automation, waits for a person to approve it. Approval is the hand: the arch becomes a loop, a person’s work.

Watar has no face, no sparkle, no glow and no colour of its own. It is endorsed by Iqaa and never appears as a separate product.

Co-branding

Tenant first, Iqaa signs off

The organization comes first

On an organization’s subdomain, in the link its clients open and in the messages they receive, the organization’s name leads. Iqaa signs off once, at the foot, with “Runs on Iqaa”.

An organization with its own logo shows it. One without gets its signature as an avatar, written from its own line.
