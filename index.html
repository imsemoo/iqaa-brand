Iqaa · brand identity

Work, in time.

Iqaa runs an organization’s work as a line: people and automatic steps, in order, so that no work falls between two hands. Its identity writes that line with one pen — straight where a step runs by itself, a loop where a person’s hand does the work — so every organization’s line becomes its own signature.

In the product

Not painted on: it is how the product speaks

Every screen uses the same strokes to say where work stands: a loop for what a person did, a dashed grey pen for what is not real yet, a metro map for the whole line. Here, Reem’s day at Sufra on a laptop and Sufra’s line on a phone.

The idea

One pen, four strokes

RunsA step that runs by itself: a message, a reminder, an invoice. A straight stroke, ink.
WaitsThe line waits for a person: an approval, a review, a Watar draft. An open arch, violet.
By handA person does the work. A loop of the hand, violet.
PauseA timed wait. The pen lifts and leaves a dot, ink.

The stroke carries the meaning, so a line reads in one colour, in greyscale and for colour-blind eyes. Colour adds one more fact: violet is always a person. Between stations the pen runs a short straight: the handoff.

Every line signs its own name

Stations from the client deck. The steps inside each station are illustrative, built only from step types the plan names. Beside each line, the same line signed on the organization’s tile: its mark. Sufra, Raqm and Kadr are demo organizations.
