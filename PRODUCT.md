# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

This repository is a static HTML/CSS/JS prototype of the identity and the first screens; pages are generated by `tools/build.py`. Production is Laravel 13 with React (the client's build plan), Arabic and English, on organization subdomains.

## Users

- **Organization owners** who sign up, name their organization, pick its field and approve the line every job will walk. They are running a kitchen, a software house or a studio, not configuring software for a living.
- **The people who work the line** (the demo cast: Reem on quotes, Salma on prep, Yusuf on delivery, Hala). They sign in on their organization's subdomain, often on a phone, to see what is waiting for them today.
- **Clients** of those organizations, who approve a quote or ask for a change from a link, without an account.

## Product Purpose

Iqaa gives any organization one production line for its work: a sequence of stations every job walks through, with checklists that lock at each handoff, steps that run by themselves between stations (send, wait, remind, condition, create, move), and tasks for people. Success for phase 1 is a pilot organization running one order through its own line from start to finish without help.

## Positioning

Automation tools (n8n, Zapier, Make) connect apps; work boards (ClickUp, Monday, Asana) list tasks. Iqaa coordinates a sequence of people and automatic steps over time and shows it as one line, so no work falls between two hands. Its assistant, Watar, drafts and always waits for a person to approve.

## Operating Context

- Each organization lives on its own subdomain (`sufra.iqaa.app`); people sign in there. The root domain hosts the platform and its administration.
- Sign-up (decided 2026-11-01): self-serve sign-up is designed now (the build plan schedules it for phase 3; in phase 1 an admin creates the organization and invites its owner, who then skips the account step).
- Fields offered at sign-up (decided 2026-11-01): catering, software company and video production, whose stations come from the client deck, plus "something else", where Watar drafts a line from the owner's own words.
- Lines have versions: draft and publish; running jobs keep the version they started on. Test runs send nothing.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Sign-in is email and password from the Laravel starter, in the product's own design; no single sign-on is planned.
- Navigation of the app: Today, Line, Orders, Clients, Settings, Ask Watar.
- Arabic is the product's first language and right-to-left; this round is English first, built with logical properties so Arabic and Turkish follow.
- Most features are planned, not built. Nothing may be presented as live.

## Brand Commitments

The identity book in this repository is binding: the name Iqaa; the signature grammar, one pen stroke (straight = a step that runs, loop = a person does it, open arch = waits for a person, pen lift and dot = a timed wait, half a step of straight between stations); violet means a person and everything automatic is ink; Atkinson Hyperlegible Next and Fragment Mono; beat 16 px, tempo 100 BPM; no gradient, glass, glow or sparkle; Watar's mark is the held arch; on an organization's surfaces the organization leads and Iqaa signs off with "Runs on Iqaa". The Line screen draws the line as a metro map (decided 2026-11-01): even spacing, every station an interchange ring, branches at 45°, ways back as square U-turns at half weight; the signature stays the line's compact name. Every decision and its evidence: `decisions.html`.

## Product Principles

1. Work is a line, not a pile.
2. Nothing moves without a handoff, and a handoff can be taken back.
3. Machines draft; people decide.
4. Try it without consequences: a test sends nothing.
5. Learn by working: a real (or test) job instead of a tour.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. States are told apart by shape, never by colour alone. Targets at least 44 px. Phone first for the people who work the line.
